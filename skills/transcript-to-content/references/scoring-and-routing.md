# Topic and Short-Form Video Scoring

Apply these scores only after transcript coverage, ownership, confidentiality, owner recognition, strategic fit, evidence, and fatigue gates have run. A strong score cannot rescue a failed gate.

## Topic Score

| Dimension | Points | Question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Source fidelity | 15 | Is the idea and its wording clearly supported by the complete transcript? |
| Owner recognition | 10 | Does a nontechnical owner immediately recognize the situation and stakes? |
| Strategic-initiative alignment | 10 | Does it materially advance the client's current initiative? |
| Pillar and brand alignment | 10 | Does it fit one client-defined pillar and reinforce positioning? |
| Audience and business relevance | 10 | Does it solve a meaningful owner problem rather than explain a mechanism? |
| Distinctive client POV | 10 | Is the reframe original, specific, and owned by the client? |
| Specificity and proof | 10 | Does it contain a source-supported mechanism, example, number, or lived observation? |
| Human connection | 10 | Does it connect the founder experience to a concrete owner outcome? |
| Marketing authority | 5 | Is there a defensible idea worth discussing with a credible peer or amplifier? |
| Demand evidence quality | 5 | Is relevance supported by dated, attributable evidence with limits stated? |
| Utility and conversation potential | 5 | Can the audience apply, save, repeat, or respond substantively? |

### Interpretation

- 85 to 100: flagship idea
- 75 to 84: strong publishable idea
- 60 to 74: useful with sharper framing
- Below 60: hold or discard

Transcript language proves qualitative relevance and phrasing, not search volume, trend status, or broad demand. If demand research is unavailable, award no evidence-quality points and label the gap.

## Fatigue risk gate

Compare this fingerprint with parsed content history:

`pillar + owner_problem + unique_pov + hook_mechanism + format + primary_cta_class`

Use the explicit batch review date for cooldown calculations. Do not invent a publishing date for an unscheduled topic.

- **Clear:** No known conflict in available history.
- **Review:** A partial overlap exists, but new proof, buyer stage, or conclusion may justify it.
- **Hold:** The same problem, POV, and hook combination is inside the cooldown or a manifest limit would be breached.
- **Unknown:** History is unavailable or incomplete. Never label this `clear`.

## Short-Form Video Score

| Dimension | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Strong spoken delivery or performance potential | 20 |
| Audible recognition in the first sentence | 20 |
| Emotion, tension, or story | 15 |
| Visual demonstration or expressive presence | 15 |
| Compact payoff | 15 |
| Broad founder relatability | 10 |
| Remix, response, or comment potential | 5 |

Score video from 0 to 100. A topic is video-eligible at 65 or higher only when the complete source supports a founder-led recording and no gate fails.

For every candidate, return Topic Score, Video Score, and one concise video rationale. Do not calculate image or carousel scores.

## Delivery calibration

Use original founder-led delivery. Begin from a beat map, preserve natural clauses and sentence-length variation, identify must-land ideas, and leave room to improvise. Use on-screen text only when it improves recognition, context, or accessibility.
